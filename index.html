Dear Bev,

You were our very first friend in the United States, and you made this new, unfamiliar country feel like home from the very beginning. You treated us with so much kindness, and made life here — for us and for our families — just a little bit easier. We love you so, so much.

You are such a genuinely kind person. To invite two international students into your home for Thanksgiving, and then turn around and make them your family, forever, giving them your love without asking for anything back — that isn't something everyone has the heart to do. You did. And you'll always hold an enormous place in our hearts.

The places we explored together, the foods we tasted for the very first time with you, the experiences we shared, the stories about the mountains that you and Jim taught us — they all hold the most special place in our hearts.

You were our first, best, and truest friend in the US — someone we could always, always count on. Thank you for being exactly who you are.

♡ ♡ ♡

Stay safe. Stay healthy. We miss you and love you more than words can say.

With all our love,
Yoshita & Sowjanya
